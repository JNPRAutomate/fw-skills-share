## How to contribute

Contributors are credited in skill frontmatter `author` fields through the `CONTRIBUTING_AUTHORS` constant in `scripts/check-skill-packages.py`. Add yourself to that list in your pull request, and see [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/mechubsec/fwskillsshare/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for submission guidelines.
